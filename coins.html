Geiger Edelmetalle

World-class craftsmanship and advanced security.

A Collector's Favorite

Geiger bars are known for world-class anti-counterfeiting features. Their bars feature reeded edges and a proprietary invisible varnish on the reverse side. When exposed to ultraviolet light, an illuminated LEV rhombus and the minting year become visible.

These advanced security features, combined with exceptional craftsmanship and elegant designs, have made Geiger products favorites among investors and collectors worldwide.

Agoro Blind Boxes

Limited releases with a surprise inside every box.

Limited Releases. Endless Surprises.

Agoro Blind Boxes have quickly become some of the most exciting collectibles featured during Coin City livestreams. Each blind box contains a surprise collectible from an officially licensed franchise, making every opening a unique experience.

Coin City receives only a limited quantity of each release, so these boxes often sell out quickly. Whether you are completing a collection, searching for a rare chase piece, or hoping to uncover real gold, every opening is a luck-of-the-draw experience.
